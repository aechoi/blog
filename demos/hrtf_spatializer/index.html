Polyauralizer

Load an audio file or record from your microphone, then drag out a trajectory to hear it move around your head in real time. Touch/Latch controls what happens when you release a position control: Touch follows whatever's already recorded, Latch holds your last value going forward until you touch that channel again. Hotkeys: A/D or ←/→ for azimuth, W/S or ↑/↓ for elevation, Q/E to move the source in/out, Space toggles play/pause.
